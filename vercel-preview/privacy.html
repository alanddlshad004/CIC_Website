Privacy Policy

At Comprehensive Interventional Center (CIC), we respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you visit our website or use our services. By accessing or using this website, you agree to the terms outlined in this policy.

1. Information We Collect

We collect various types of information to provide better services to our visitors. The types of data we collect include:

  • Personal Identification Information: Name, email address, phone number, and any other information you provide through forms or communications.
  • Non-Personal Identification Information: Browser type, device information, IP address, and site usage data to improve our website's functionality and user experience.
  • Health-Related Information: Only if voluntarily provided for the purpose of consultation or health-related inquiries. This information is handled with the utmost confidentiality.

2. How We Use Your Information

The information we collect is used to:

  • Provide you with the best possible medical services, information, and updates about CIC.
  • Respond to inquiries, process appointment requests, and offer customer support.
  • Enhance our website’s functionality and improve the user experience.
  • Communicate promotional offers, treatments, and updates (if you have opted to receive them).
  • Comply with legal obligations and ensure the proper operation of our services.

3. Protection of Your Information

We employ strict security measures to protect your personal information. These measures include encryption, secure servers, and restricted access to your data. CIC is committed to ensuring the security and confidentiality of your personal data.

4. Sharing Your Information

CIC does not sell, rent, or lease your personal information. However, we may share your information with trusted third-party service providers to assist with website hosting, customer service, or payment processing. These third parties are bound by strict confidentiality agreements and are only permitted to use your data for the specific services they provide to CIC.

5. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

Our website doesnt uses cookies and similar tracking technologies to enhance Security of user. Cookies are small text files stored on your device. You can manage your cookie preferences through your browser settings.

6. External Links

Our website may contain links to external websites that are not controlled by CIC. These third-party sites have their own privacy policies, which we encourage you to review. CIC is not responsible for the privacy practices or content of external sites.

7. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Request access to the personal information we hold about you.
  • Request updates or corrections to any inaccurate data.
  • Request deletion of your personal data (under applicable law).
  • Withdraw consent for us to use your personal data at any time.

If you wish to exercise any of these rights, please contact us at info@ci-c.org

8. Data Retention

We will retain your personal information only for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes for which it was collected, or as required by law.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

CIC reserves the right to modify this Privacy Policy at any time. Any changes will be posted on this page, and the updated date will be reflected at the top of the page. By continuing to use our website, you acknowledge and agree to the revised policy.

10. Governing Law

This Privacy Policy is governed by the laws of the jurisdiction in which CIC operates. Any disputes arising out of the use of this website or the privacy policy will be resolved in the courts located in that jurisdiction.
