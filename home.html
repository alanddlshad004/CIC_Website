Our Impact

We’re committed to improving lives through advanced medical interventions— for our community, our patients, our professionals, and the future of healthcare.

Our Actions

Working Together to Prevent, Treat, and Recover from Stroke:

Leadership in Stroke Care

Our commitment to leadership in stroke care includes:
  • Delivering cutting-edge endovascular treatments for stroke
  • Ensuring timely and precise interventions to save lives
  • Promoting patient safety and recovery through comprehensive care
  • Upholding the highest ethical and clinical standards

Comprehensive Stroke Care

Our commitment to advanced stroke treatment includes:
  • Emergency treatment for ischemic and hemorrhagic strokes
  • Minimally invasive procedures such as thrombectomy and clot retrieval
  • Innovative techniques like coiling and flow diversion to restore blood flow

Aneurysm Treatment

Our commitment to treating brain aneurysms includes:
  • Utilizing advanced endovascular techniques such as coiling
  • Employing flow diversion methods to ensure long-term success
  • Minimizing recovery time with minimally invasive procedures
